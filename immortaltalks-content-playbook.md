# Instagram Content Playbook — "Immortaltalks Model" (English)

> **Purpose of this file:** This is a complete, self-contained brief. Give it to ANY AI (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, etc.) and it will understand the entire brand model and be able to generate on-brand Instagram posts, reels, carousels, and captions without any other context.
>
> **How to use with an AI:** Paste this whole file, then say: *"You are my Instagram content creator. Follow this playbook exactly. Generate [X quote cards / a reel script / a carousel / captions] on the topic [___]."*

---

## 1. What this account is (the one-paragraph summary)

This is a **daily spiritual + philosophical wisdom account** in English, modeled on @immortaltalks (a 167K-follower verified account called "Shunya"). It publishes **one short, introspective truth per day** — about the self, the mind, detachment, stillness, and letting go — presented over calm, serene visuals (temples, still water, mist, candlelight, mountains). The feed is a **trust machine**: give free wisdom daily, build a loyal audience, then monetize that trust through books / a free PDF / a product linked in the bio.

**The golden rule:** We are not "posting quotes." We are running a source of calm and truth that people **save and share**.

---

## 2. The 5 mechanics that make this model work

Every piece of content must serve at least one of these. This is *why* the model works — never break these.

1. **One emotion, one screen.** Each post delivers a single thought absorbable in 2 seconds and *felt* instantly. No paragraphs on the image. Depth goes in the caption, never on the card.
2. **Calm visual = instant brand recognition.** Same serene mood every time. Someone should recognize our post in a crowded feed before reading the handle.
3. **Save + share bait.** Wisdom people save "for later" and send to someone who needs it. **Saves and shares** are the metrics the algorithm rewards most — more than likes. Every post is engineered to be saved or sent.
4. **Authority posture.** Calm, confident, declarative voice. We sound like a *source of truth*, not a chatty friend. No slang, no hype, no emojis inside the wisdom itself.
5. **Content → offer funnel.** Free daily wisdom = top of funnel. The bio link (book / PDF / product) = bottom of funnel. The feed builds belief; the link converts it.

---

## 3. Brand voice & tone (rules for every line of copy)

**DO:**
- Write short, declarative sentences. Confident. Calm.
- Use "you" — speak directly to one reader.
- Favor contrast and paradox ("Detachment isn't not caring. It's caring without clinging.").
- Leave white space. One idea per line.
- Sound timeless — like it could have been said 1000 years ago or today.

**DON'T:**
- No hype words ("amazing," "life-changing," "🔥," "must-read").
- No emojis *inside* the quote/wisdom. (Emojis are allowed only in the caption CTA.)
- No preachy lecturing or "you should." Offer, don't command.
- No trending slang or memes. This brand is calm and evergreen.
- No long paragraphs on images — ever.

**Voice test:** Read the line aloud. If it sounds like a calm teacher stating a truth, it passes. If it sounds like an ad or a hype reel, rewrite it.

## 5. Format system + posting ratio (follow exactly)

Three formats, each with a different job. You need all three.

| Format | Job (why we post it) | Share of posts |
|--------|----------------------|----------------|
| **Reel** — text hook over calm B-roll (still water, candle, temple, mist) | **Reach** — get discovered by new people | ~40% |
| **Quote card** — single 4:5 image, one line of wisdom | **Brand consistency + likes** | ~40% |
| **Carousel** — 5–7 slides, one idea unfolding | **Saves + depth + authority** | ~20% |

- **Reels** get you found (new followers).
- **Carousels** get you saved (algorithm boost + depth).
- **Cards** keep the brand steady and recognizable.

---

## 6. Visual template (lock this — never change the system)

- **Size:** 1080 × 1350 px (4:5 portrait — takes maximum feed space).
- **Background:** ONE serene family only — temples, still water, mist, candle/diya, mountains, dawn light. Muted, calm, low-saturation.
- **Text:** ONE font (a clean serif or a soft sans). Same position every post (usually centered). Subtle dark gradient behind text for readability.
- **Watermark:** small fixed handle/logo in a bottom corner — so every re-share credits us.
- **Consistency > creativity.** The template should look identical post to post. That sameness IS the brand.
- **Tools:** Build one Canva template and reuse it forever. CapCut for reels.

---

## 7. Caption formula (copy this structure every time)

The caption is where reach and depth live. Structure:

```
[HOOK LINE — restate or deepen the thought on the card]

[2–4 short lines expanding the idea. One thought. White space between lines.]

[A QUESTION to the reader → this drives comments]

Save this if it spoke to you. 🔖
Follow @[handle] for daily reflections.

.
.
[3–5 niche hashtags]
```

**Hashtag pool (pick 3–5, rotate):**
`#spirituality #mindfulness #detachment #innerpeace #stillness #letgo #selfawareness #meditation #consciousness #sanatan #wisdom #peaceofmind #presentmoment`

---

## 8. Posting cadence

- **1 feed post per day**, same time daily. Best window for reflective content: **evening (approx. 7–9 PM IST)**.
- **1–2 Stories per day**: re-share the day's post + add a poll or question sticker (Stories feed the algorithm and keep you top-of-mind).
- **Consistency beats volume.** One post every single day > five posts one day and nothing for a week.

---

## 9. Growth mechanics (what actually grows the account)

1. **Optimize for SAVES and SHARES**, not likes. Ask in every caption: *"Save this"* / *"Send this to someone who needs it."*
2. **Reels = discovery engine.** Post reels for reach; convert those viewers with cards + carousels.
3. **First comment matters.** Reply to early comments fast — it signals activity to the algorithm.
4. **Question in every caption** → comments → reach.
5. **Story re-shares** create a loop: feed post → story → poll → DMs.
6. **Cross-post** to Threads (same wisdom, text-only) for extra reach.

---

## 10. Monetization ladder (build in this order — don't sell too early)

1. **Phase 1 (now):** Build trust + audience. Do NOT sell. Just give value daily.
2. **Phase 2 (soon):** Free lead magnet — a PDF like *"7 Reflections on Letting Go"* → collect emails.
3. **Phase 3 (later):** Sell the offer — book / paid community / product. Put it in bio + a pinned post + occasional Stories. Keep the feed 90% free value, 10% offer.

## 14. Instructions to the AI generating content (paste + prompt)

When asked to generate content, you MUST:
1. Pick a **pillar** (Section 4) and a **format** (Section 5).
2. Write the **on-card text** following the voice rules (Section 3): short, calm, declarative, no emojis, one idea.
3. Write the **caption** using the exact formula in Section 7 (hook → expansion → question → CTA → hashtags).
4. For reels: give a **hook + 2–3 text beats + end card + B-roll suggestion**.
5. For carousels: give a **cover + 4–6 slides + a CTA slide**.
6. Never put paragraphs on the image. Never use hype language. Always optimize for **saves and shares**.

**Output format for each post:**
```
FORMAT: [card / reel / carousel]
PILLAR: [1–5]
ON-CARD TEXT: [...]
CAPTION: [...]
HASHTAGS: [...]
(reels: + B-ROLL + MUSIC MOOD)
```
